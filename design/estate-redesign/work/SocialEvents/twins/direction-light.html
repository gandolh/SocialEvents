The open invitation

Tonight's event sits on top of the calendar as a raised ticket with its two answers, over a full-width month on the Aero blue desktop, with glass kept for the bars.

Palette

By role, light and dark side by side. Every value is used in the screens. Aero's blue and category hues are today's, tuned for contrast.
